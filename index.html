About

Faith, scholarship and communication

Jeshurun Elvis Awuah is a Ghanaian theologian whose work moves between the pulpit, the study and the studio. He holds a Bachelor of Theology with First Class Honours from Trinity Theological Seminary and a Master of Philosophy in the Study of Religions (Biblical Studies) from the University of Ghana.

He has served in pastoral ministry since 2007, preaching, teaching, counselling and building church structures in both Accra and Kumasi. Alongside ministry, he has led media and brand work for churches, produced and hosted programmes, and written and edited for other researchers through a private research consultancy.

His academic interests lie in Biblical studies, African theology and African Christianity, New Testament theology, Biblical Greek, and the ways modern tools can carry the church's message to more people.

Sermon · March 22, 2026

Meditating on God's Word

Preached at the Sunday Glory Service of Dominion Chapel International (The Special Place), this message draws on Psalm 119 to show how closeness to God's word becomes a source of safety and guidance, even when danger or opposition surrounds us.

  • Safety in the word: real security grows from an intimate relationship with Scripture.
  • Turning from vanities: fame, status and possessions fade, while God's statutes endure.
  • Overcoming dread: trust God's promises rather than giving in to fear.
  • The value of God's law: it gives wisdom, light and an edge in life.
